Download for Android

Works on almost every Android phone

Slow or costly data? Get the smaller download for phones from 2019 or newer.

Your phone will show a warning. That's normal. Android warns about every app downloaded from a website instead of Google Play. The AllBioHub app comes straight from us, is signed by AllBioHub, and doesn't ask for your contacts, photos or location.

How to install (about a minute)

  1. Tap Download for Android above. If Chrome says "This type of file can harm your device", tap Download anyway.
  2. When the download finishes, tap Open. (Missed it? Open your Files or Downloads app and tap allbiohub.)
  3. If your phone says "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source", tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then tap back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect asks to scan the app, tap Scan app, and when it's done, tap Install.
  5. Tap Open. That's it. You'll find AllBioHub with your other apps.

Questions

Is it safe?

Yes. The warnings appear for every app installed from outside Google Play, not because of anything in AllBioHub. The app only needs internet access (and asks before sending notifications, if you turn them on). Every version is signed with the same AllBioHub key, so your phone will only accept updates that really come from us. We're also bringing the app to Google Play.

It says "App not installed"

If you used the smaller download, your phone may be an older 32-bit model: use the main Download for Android button instead. Also check you have some free storage space, and that the download finished completely (try downloading it again on Wi-Fi).

Samsung, Xiaomi, Tecno, Infinix or itel phone?

The steps are the same, but the setting in step 3 may be called Install unknown apps or Unknown sources. If the phone opens its own security check, let it finish and tap Install or Continue.

How do I update?

Download and install the newest version from this page the same way. It installs over the old one and keeps your saved stories and settings.

Can I check the file is genuine?

Each release lists the SHA-256 checksum of every file in SHA256SUMS.txt.
